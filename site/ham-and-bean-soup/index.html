Ham and Bean Soup

Prep10 mins Cook3 hrs Total3 hrs 10 mins Servings13 cups CourseSoup

Ingredients

  • 2–3 Tbspbacon fat, unsalted
  • 1 Tbspunsalted butter
  • 1 largeyellow onion, diced
  • 1 (8 oz.)ham steak, diced small
  • 3 largecarrots, dicedAbout 10–12 oz. — match the weight of the diced onion.
  • 2 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 TbspBetter Than Bouillon, chicken flavor
  • 8 cupswater
  • 1ham hockIf thawed, bag it before it goes in the pot.
  • 1 bagdried navy beans
  • 2 tsponion powder
  • 4bay leaves
  • 4 sprigsthyme
  • 4 sprigsrosemary
  • 4 leavessage, diced
  • 2 tspliquid smoke

Instructions

  1. Sauté the bacon fat in the butter until soft.

  2. Add the ham, garlic, and sage and sauté. Stir in the liquid smoke.

  3. Add the onions and cook until soft and translucent.

  4. Add half the carrots and cook until soft. Save the other half to add later for better consistency.

  5. Add the broth, water (save out about 2 cups for the beans), ham hock, onion powder, and the remaining herbs. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.

  6. One hour after the boil starts, add the beans along with the 2 cups cold water.

  7. Simmer with the lid partially cracked for at least 3 hours to fully cook. Stir occasionally; the beans settle on the bottom.

    Add the reserved carrots for the last 40–45 minutes.

  8. Remove the ham hock, bay leaves, and thyme and rosemary stems. Serve and enjoy!
